## Generated content

Generated by the game's own code (`src/sprite_gen.cpp`, `src/assets.cpp`, `src/audio.cpp`) and covered by its [MIT licence](LICENSE):

- street furniture, the metro train and building window masks;
- light, flare and smoke textures;
- all sound effects.

### CJ-040 Asphalt Rock FM

The songs, the DJ talk and the commercials of the first radio station (`assets/radio/asphalt_rock/`) were made by the project's author with [Suno](https://suno.com/) on 2026-10-11 and 2026-10-12, without third-party audio. The files, their Suno titles and IDs are listed in the [station's README](assets/radio/asphalt_rock/README.md). They were made on a paid Suno plan (Pro or Premier), under whose terms the author owns the output and may distribute it, commercially as well; the author distributes them with the game. No third-party licence is claimed for them.

### CJ-004 source sprite sheets

The [motorbike source art](assets/art/cj004/README.md) was generated with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-10-07 without a third-party image input; the user accepted the designs, and the game uses their six exports (`assets/vehicles/*-empty.png` and `*-ridden.png`). The generated civilian sheets and idle masters were rejected and, with the old player sprites, removed on 2026-10-11 ([ADR-0009](docs/adr/0009-required-character-atlases.md)); they remain in the Git history. The exact [generation and edit prompts](assets/art/cj004/prompts.json) are preserved. No third-party CC0 licence is claimed for the generated artwork.

### CJ-030 loading screen wordmark

The user supplied the [original wordmark](docs/images/cj030-loading-wordmark-source.png): white cracked-concrete CONCRETE lettering, red graffiti JUNGLE and a red crown on magenta. Its original creation tool is not recorded. The source SHA-256 is `260dc6f25ee5e9d089310f942bcd887c08b5876d8915b7f8ca5dc6ced73c0703`. The built-in OpenAI ImageGen edit tool prepared a transparent cutout on 2026-10-13; it was a background-extraction edit of the supplied design, not a new artwork generation. The [exact prompt](docs/design/loading-wordmark-prompt.txt) and [asset record](docs/design/loading-screen-art-brief.md#wordmark) preserve the request and preparation details.

The first cutout was integrated on 2026-10-14 as the transparent [runtime wordmark](assets/ui/loading-logo.png), SHA-256 `02e0bcf4abb66faf39586b184ed67d869d606d6342066f15ad5201acf8753002`. The runtime file preserves that cutout's bytes; its foreground is visually consistent with the supplied design, without a pixel-identity claim. No third-party asset licence or original generator is asserted for either supplied image.
